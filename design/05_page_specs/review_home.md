# Review Home Page Spec

## Page Role
The Review page is the **long-term training intelligence panel** of FitTracker.

This page proves that the app is not only useful during a session, but also valuable across weeks and months.

---

## Primary UX Goal
The user should quickly understand:
1. whether progress is happening
2. what the important trends are
3. what their strongest records are
4. what part of their training history deserves attention

The page should support interpretation, not just storage.

---

## Core Visual Goal
The Review page should feel like a **premium personal performance dashboard**.
Not a backend admin list. Not a generic analytics report. Not a card dump.

It should feel:
- analytical
- refined
- stable
- high-value
- easy to scan

---

## Information Hierarchy

### Level 1: Progress summary
This is the first impression of whether the user is moving forward.
Examples:
- training consistency
- weekly trend
- completion quality
- key progress signal

### Level 2: High-value records and trend panels
This includes:
- PR areas
- weekly trend areas
- meaningful stats
- recent progress patterns

### Level 3: History and supporting detail
This includes:
- recent sessions
- filter controls
- supporting lists
- secondary metrics

These should remain understandable but quieter than the main trend and record signals.

---

## Required Aesthetic Outcomes
The page should not look like:
- a dark dashboard template
- a backend reporting page
- a cluttered panel stack
- a long history list with weak hierarchy

The page should look like:
- a high-end training data panel
- a deliberate long-term progress surface
- a place where records and trends feel meaningful

---

## Data Presentation Philosophy
This page is where FitTracker earns trust.
Every major block should help answer one of these:
- Am I consistent?
- Am I getting stronger?
- What changed recently?
- Which training data matters most?

If a block does not support interpretation, it should be reduced, merged, or demoted.

---

## Color and Surface Rules
- Stronger surfaces should be reserved for truly important summary blocks.
- Green should indicate positive state, active focus, trend direction, and key data emphasis.
- Gold should be rare and reserved for exceptional value moments like PR or standout performance.
- Silver / muted system tones should support chart and structural clarity.

---

## Chart and Trend Rules
- Charts should feel calm and premium, not noisy.
- Heatmap intensity should be easy to understand at a glance.
- Trend panels should privilege readability over decoration.
- PR and recent performance should stand out, but not through loud visual tricks.

---

## Page Success Criteria
The Review redesign is successful only if:
- the page feels more valuable than a plain history list
- the user can identify the most important training signals quickly
- trends, records, and history have clear hierarchy
- the page feels premium and analytical rather than generic and cluttered
- the page visually belongs to the same product family as Home and Active Workout

---

## Failure Signs
The redesign is not acceptable if:
- the page still feels like a dark backend dashboard
- the history list dominates the screen too early
- charts feel decorative rather than useful
- PR and high-value information are not meaningfully emphasized
- the page has more polish but not more interpretive power
